1. Why an entity (ethos-linked)

Without an entity: no durable covenant signature, weak production key ceremony party, difficult bank/KYC (T7), awkward commercial SOWs (T11), succession ambiguity. The free public core can still exist in git; stewardship instruments cannot be fully load-bearing.

3. Formation sequence (executable checklist)

  1. Engage counsel; choose jurisdiction consciously (tax, liability, investor familiarity).
  2. Name availability / reservation.
  3. File incorporation with corporate regulator.
  4. Obtain tax identifiers as required.
  5. Open business bank account (hard gate T7 — KYC).
  6. Adopt constitution; attach Boundary + Covenant schedules.
  7. IP assignment: free_core software, site content, brand, domain beneficial notes.
  8. Issue shares; first board minutes (see §4).
  9. Public status note on ttllms.com: entity formed (date, jurisdiction; no private home addresses).
  10. Domain 1 decision log entry with options considered.
  11. Update continuity/ASSET_INVENTORY.md primary holder → Entity.
  12. Plan production key ceremony (T5) and second custodian (T9).

4. First board resolutions (example language)

Resolved that:

(a) The company adopts the Free Public Core Boundary (commercial/BOUNDARY.md) as binding internal policy.

(b) Demo signing keys are not production roots of trust.

(c) md@0265.au remains operational public contact until officers expand.

(d) Second custodian appointment is a priority workstream.

(e) No officer may execute a close-core side letter.

(f) Public verify harness and seal freshness remain release gates for free-core claims.
