Master Services Agreement — Hosted Infrastructure SKU (full example)

Written by Grok - Human checking required
Commercial counsel voice. Not an executable contract. Hard gates T6/T7/T11 for real sales.
Free Public Core carve-out is the load-bearing clause.

1. Parties and contact

Provider: [TTLLM Entity] — md@0265.au

Customer: [Legal name]

2. Recitals (ethos)

Provider publishes Free Public Core artefacts that anyone may inspect without payment. Customer wishes optional hosted reliability, capacity, and support. The parties agree that hosted services do not purchase exclusivity over Free Public Core or paywalled verification of public claims.

3. Services

Provider shall provide hosted access to agreed models/workloads with service levels, monitoring, and support as set in an Order Form. Underlying public model weights and verification tools remain available under their public terms independently of this Agreement.

4. Free Public Core carve-out (mandatory)

4.1 Nothing in this Agreement paywalls verification of public-core claims, basic public ttlink for public models, or access to public manifests.

4.2 Provider shall not require Customer to accept free-core closure as a condition of service.

4.3 Public model weights remain governed by public licenses/terms, not by MSA fees.

4.4 Order of precedence: public Boundary and free-core licenses control over this MSA on Free Public Core questions.

5. Customer data and isolation

5.1 Customer prompts and private documents are Customer confidential.

5.2 Customer data shall not be trained into Free Public Core without explicit separate written licence and Domain 3 process.

5.3 Provider maintains separation of tenant systems from public release signing keys and public index mutation paths (isolation principles in commercial/ISOLATION_RUNBOOK.md).

6. Lineage for any fine-tunes

If services include fine-tunes marketed with TTLLM inheritance, Provider shall publish lineage to public base merkle roots without leaking Customer private data (finetune_lineage schema).

7. Fees, term, liability

[HUMAN commercial schedules] — pricing may be private; public capability claims used in marketing must still pass Domain 4 claim gate.

8. Security research

Good-faith research against public free-core artefacts follows public intake; this MSA does not silence Domain 10 publication of free-core integrity findings.
