Compensation philosophy and bands — full example

Written by Grok - Human checking required
People/ops voice for Domain 6. Bands are illustrative AUD FTE pre-revenue. Not offers of employment.
Influence disclosure is the load-bearing requirement; secrecy of influential roles is soft tissue.

1. Principles (ethos)

  1. Roles that can merge free_core, hold production signing keys, edit BOUNDARY, or deploy public green claims are above influence threshold → disclosable role (name/handle when staffed).
  2. Public bands for influential roles once payroll exists; exact individual pay may stay private within band.
  3. Sales incentives must not reward free-core closure or paywalled verification.
  4. Standing adversarial (CCO) is paid for finding truth, not for silence.
