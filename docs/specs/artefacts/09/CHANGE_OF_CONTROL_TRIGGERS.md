# Change of control triggers (Domain 9)

> **Written by Grok - Human checking required** for expanded counsel language in
> `docs/placeholders/legal/COVENANT_FULL_EXAMPLE.md`.

## Triggers (public)
1. Sale or transfer of majority voting power of the TTLLM entity (once formed).
2. Exclusive license preventing public redistribution of free public core artefacts.
3. Investor or customer side letter requiring free-core closure or paywalled verification.
4. Transfer of both domains (ttllms.com and ttllms.org) to a party that refuses BOUNDARY.
5. Replacement of production signing policy that reverts to single-person demo keys as sole trust root without Domain 1 log.

## Required responses
- Domain 1 decision within 7 days
- Public status note
- BOUNDARY re-attestation if commercial activity exists
- Incident path if integrity of already-public artefacts is threatened

## Non-triggers
- Routine officer appointments that preserve BOUNDARY
- Hosting vendor changes that do not alter free-core publication
