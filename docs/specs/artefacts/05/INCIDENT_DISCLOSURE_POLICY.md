# Incident & Failure Disclosure Policy (v0.1)

## Thresholds (material)
- Integrity failure of public manifests, ttlink index, or stream tip
- Security incident affecting public core or signing keys
- Data corruption or silent alteration of public artefacts
- Prolonged public site / stream unavailability (>24h) affecting proof surfaces

## Timelines
- Initial acknowledgement: within 72 hours of confirmation
- Fuller technical disclosure / post-mortem: within 30 days
- Security exemption: time-limited, logged, renewable; permanent secrecy forbidden

## Scope
Applies to models and transparency infrastructure equally.
