## Summary

A TTLLM goes beyond open weights: full stack for reproducibility and inspection — weights, training code, training data (or precise composition + access path), intermediate checkpoints, hyperparameters, metrics/logs, and tools to trace outputs to data and inspect behaviour (ttlink).

## Why (ethos)

Open-weight alone is soft-tissue marketing. Totally transparent means nowhere left to hide the computational skeleton.

## Prerequisites

- Shared definition accepted by maintainers

## What to do (executable instructions)

1. Publish the definition on ttllms.com and in manifesto — no watering down.
2. Maintain a comparison table: closed / open-weight / process-transparent / TTLLM.
3. For every model release, publish a compliance checklist against this definition.
4. Reject branding a release 'TTLLM' if any required layer is missing without a public tombstone reason.
5. Update glossary when terms drift; keep bone metaphors consistent with culture docs.
