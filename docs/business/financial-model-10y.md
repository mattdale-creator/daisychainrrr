# 10-year financial model (assumptions)

**Updated:** 2026-08-07

## Founding order-of-magnitude (re-verify with live quotes)
- 32B-class full project: ~$4–8M (training + eng + data + red team + storage)  
- 7–13B first public release: substantially less  

## Cost centers
- Training generations (FLOPs, storage, checkpoints)  
- Production ttlink index (Epoch 03)  
- Free-core hosting (stream, site, manifests)  
- Red team / legal  

## Revenue (outer only)
- Hosted SLA, enterprise service, TaaS-style audit tools  

## Scenarios
1. **Lean research:** small models, grant/compute partners, minimal sales  
2. **Growth:** outer revenue funds 32B-class generation  
3. **Constrained:** capital drought — maintain free core + architecture, delay training  

Do not present only upside. Recompute when hardware prices change.
