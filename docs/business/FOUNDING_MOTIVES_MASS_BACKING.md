# Motives for mass backing (turn 27)

**Source:** founding conversation ASSISTANT turn 27

Yes. There are motives more potent for mass contribution of work and money, and several of them stay cleaner with the project’s internal logic.

The “vs. Sam / OpenAI” frame is effective at generating heat and rapid tribal energy. It is not the highest-leverage long-term motive, and it carries cultural side-effects that work against the “down to the bone” standard.

### Stronger mobilising motives

**1. Epistemic agency (“I can actually look”)**  
Most people currently have no way to verify what these systems are made of or how they were shaped. A working TTLLM + ttlink system changes that from a plea into a fact. The motive is: “I no longer have to take anyone’s word for it.”  
This is both emotionally powerful and directly aligned with the technical goal.

**2. Refusal of concentrated narrative power**  
A small number of organisations currently control both the most capable models *and* the dominant public story about what those models are. The counter-motive is not “defeat OpenAI” but “no single group should control the skeleton and the story.”  
This scales better than personal antagonism and still creates strong urgency.

**3. Building the reference layer**  
People contribute heavily to projects they believe will become the measuring stick. The offer is: help build the system against which future claims of transparency will be judged.  
This attracts builders and long-term capital more effectively than oppositional framing.

**4. Anti-mystification**  
Capable models produce a powerful immersive frame that feels mind-like. The project’s core act is puncturing that frame with visible structure. For many technically inclined and philosophically inclined people, this is a deep intrinsic motive — closer to “remember you’re on drugs” than to tribal conflict.

**5. Historical participation**  
“I was part of the effort that made frontier AI legible.”  
Legacy motives are extremely effective at recruiting high-commitment people when the work is real.

### Comparative potency

| Motive                         | Short-term mobilisation | Long-term coherence with ethos | Quality of people attracted |
|--------------------------------|--------------------------|--------------------------------|-----------------------------|
| Vs. Sam / OpenAI               | Very high               | Low–medium                    | Mixed, often oppositional  |
| Epistemic agency (“I can look”)| High                    | Very high                     | High                       |
| End black-box era              | High                    | High                          | High                       |
| Build the reference standard   | Medium–high             | Very high                     | Highest                    |
| Anti-mystification             | Medium                  | Highest                       | Highest                    |

### Recommendation

Lead with the structural offer, not the personal enemy:

- The public deserves the actual skeleton, not just the outputs.  
- No one should have to trust when they can verify.  
- We are building the reference implementation so the standard becomes real.

You can still note the contrast with closed frontier labs. You do not need to organise the identity of the project around defeating any particular person or company. The more the work itself is visible and excellent, the more the contrast generates itself.

That combination — strong epistemic motive + visible proof + invitation to build the reference layer — is more potent for sustained work and capital than pure antagonism, and it does not require the project to carry narrative soft tissue.
