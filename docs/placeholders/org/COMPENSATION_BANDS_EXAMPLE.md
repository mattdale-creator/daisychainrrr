# Compensation philosophy — example public bands

> **Written by Grok - Human checking required**  
> Written as if by people/ops for Domain 6. Pre-payroll numbers are **illustrative**.

## Principle
Roles with influence over free core, BOUNDARY, signing keys, or public claims are disclosable. Exact individual comp may be private below influence thresholds; **bands** for influential roles are public once payroll exists.

## Example bands (AUD, full-time equivalent, pre-revenue illustration)
| Role | Band (example) | Influence |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| Founder / project lead | deferred / draw TBD | High |
| Training lead | $120k–$180k | High if merge+release |
| CCO / standing red team | $140k–$200k or retainer | High |
| Commercial sales | $90k–$140k + commission **outside** core | Medium |
| Contractor eng (merge rights) | $800–$1500/day | High if free_core merge |

## Disclosure rule
If role can merge free_core, hold prod keys, edit BOUNDARY, or deploy public green claims → publish role + band (and name/handle when hired).

## Non-claim
These bands are not an offer of employment.
