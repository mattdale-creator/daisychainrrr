## 1. Mission

Make the transparency system as hard to deceive as the model is hard to exploit — and **publish** both classes of failure systematically. The product is the proof; false green is worse than admitted red.

---

## 2. Why this role is load-bearing

Without standing adversarial capacity, free_core can rot into soft tissue: seals that nobody attacks, indexes nobody poisons-tests, BOUNDARY that sales learns to route around. Automated harnesses (`scripts/redteam_nano_harness.py`) are bone; they are **not** a substitute for a human who treats deception of the transparency layer as a first-class bug class.

1. Own Domain 10 publication standard and findings register.
