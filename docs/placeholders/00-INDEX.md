## Why this library exists

Wherever the project said “a human must write X,” leaving a blank invited soft tissue or endless deferral. TTLLM ethos requires **load-bearing text**: free public core never paywalled; product is the proof; Domains 1–10; BOUNDARY precedence; tombstones; no fake closed gates. Grok wrote complete examples in the voice of counsel, founder, CCO, ops, and finance — using founding conversation standards (OLMo/LLM360 transparency ambition, monetise outside the bone, radical honesty).

**Human checking** means: verify facts against repo/site, localise jurisdiction/numbers/names, sign only when true, reject with line notes if wrong.
