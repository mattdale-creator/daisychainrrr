## 1. Purpose
The Entity exists to produce and steward Totally Transparent LLMs: systems whose free public core (weights and checkpoints under the TTLLM promise, training code for public releases, data composition or lawful access path, metrics, cryptographic manifests, basic ttlink, and public process stream) remains inspectable and free.

## 2. Free public core commitment
2.1 The Entity shall not paywall verification of a public-core claim.  
2.2 The Entity shall not privatise already-released free public core artefacts except via Domain 3 process (legal compulsion, safety with time-limited disclosure, or documented tombstone).  
2.3 Commercial products may exist only outside the free public core, consistent with `commercial/BOUNDARY.md`.

## 3. Transparency domains
The Entity shall maintain public processes for Domains 1–10 (governance, ownership, data, evaluation, incidents, compensation philosophy, supply chain, boundary, stewardship, red-team publication) at the standard described in `docs/specs/`.

## 4. Signing keys
4.1 Tutorial or demo keys are not production roots of trust.  
4.2 Production release signatures require multi-party or HSM-backed policy once the Entity exists.  
4.3 Compromise triggers Domain 5 incident clocks.

## 5. Change of control
5.1 Any transfer of control that could force free-core closure is a material Domain 1 event and a public disclosure event.  
5.2 Triggers include: sale of majority voting power; exclusive license that prevents public redistribution of free core; investor side letter contradicting BOUNDARY.  
5.3 See example triggers register: `docs/specs/artefacts/09/CHANGE_OF_CONTROL_TRIGGERS.md`.

## 6. Succession
6.1 Critical assets (domains, GitHub, Cloudflare, public keys, vault inventory) shall have named primary and backup custodians.  
6.2 Until a second custodian is appointed, single-human concentration risk is public (see ASSET_INVENTORY).

## 7. Annual attestation
Once revenue-bearing commercial activity begins, the Entity shall publish an annual Boundary Attestation (example: `docs/placeholders/commercial/BOUNDARY_ANNUAL_ATTESTATION_EXAMPLE.md`).

## 8. No soft tissue
Misrepresenting seal freshness, capability of nano models as frontier, or entity status as formed when unformed, is a governance failure.
