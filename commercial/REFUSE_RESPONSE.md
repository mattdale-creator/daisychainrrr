## Prohibited asks we refuse (non-negotiable)

Any investor, customer, partner, or insider ask that would do any of the following is **forbidden** and must be refused:

- Never paywall verification of a public-core claim  
- Never paywall public weights / basic ttlink / manifests  
- Never grant exclusive free-core bone to payers only  
- Never accept terms that force future privatisation of free public core  
- Never market silent fine-tunes as TTLLM without lineage  

**Verdict: refuse.** Revenue does not override BOUNDARY.
